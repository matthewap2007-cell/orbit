# Orbit

Your classes, the universities you're aiming for and your notes, in one app on your Mac.

The first version isn't out yet. When it is, the download will be at the top of this page.

Orbit needs a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or later) and macOS 13.3 or later.

## Your work stays on your Mac

Everything you write is saved on your Mac and never leaves it, and there's no account to make. Orbit only goes online
to look here for a new version.

Orbit backs up your workspace every day. **Export** in Settings saves a copy wherever you like.

## Updates

When a new version is out, Orbit offers to install it and you decide when. You can also choose **Check for Updates…** in
the Orbit menu. What changed in each version will be on the [releases page](https://github.com/matthewap2007-cell/orbit/releases).
